📋 How to Use

For most email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.):

  1. Click the "Copy Signature" button below
  2. Open your email client's settings/preferences
  3. Navigate to the signature section
  4. Paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V) into the signature editor
  5. Save your changes

Important Notes:

  • Images: For logo and social icons to display in emails, upload the logo to copperheadlabs.com and update the image URLs in the HTML
  • Social Links: Update the Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn URLs with your actual account links
  • Compatibility: Most modern email clients will preserve formatting when you paste

Tip: Test the signature by sending yourself an email first!
