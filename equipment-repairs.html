Live-Line Refinishing Process

Inspect → Clean → Patch → Sand → Paint → Seal → Dielectric Test to ASTM standards

1

Inspect

Each tool is inspected for surface wear, damage, and overall condition before work begins.

2

Clean

Dirt, oils, and contaminants are removed so patch, paint, and seal materials bond cleanly.

3

Patch

Surface defects and damaged areas are patched to restore a sound insulating surface.

4

Sand

Surfaces are sanded smooth to prepare for a consistent, durable paint finish.

5

Paint

Tools are painted to restore appearance and protect the insulating FRP surface.

6

Seal

A protective seal is applied to lock in the finish and help resist weathering in the field.

7

Dielectric Test

Finished tools are dielectric tested to ASTM standards before returning to service.

Rubber Blanket Patching

Punctures, cuts, and worn spots don't always mean a blanket is scrap. We patch rubber insulating blankets so they can return to service after inspection and dielectric testing.

Pair patching with our Rubber PPE Testing to verify repaired blankets meet ASTM requirements before they go back on the job.
